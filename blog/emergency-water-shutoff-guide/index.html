Emergency Water Shutoff Guide in Rowlett, TX

Home / Blog / Finding Your Water Shutoff Valve in Rowlett, TX

In a water damage emergency, the fastest way to minimize damage is to stop the flow of water. Finding and working the water shutoff valve is a skill that could keep thousands of dollars in your pocket and spare your property a lot of damage. If you own a home in Rowlett, TX, find your shutoff valve and practice using it now, not during a leak.

Finding the Main Shutoff Valve in Your Home

Check these spots in your Rowlett home for the main valve: along an interior garage wall, close to the foundation wall down in the basement or in a crawl space, inside the laundry area or a utility room, up front where the street line enters the house, or at the meter shutoff, which is in an access box at ground level near the street. Two types are typical: gate valves, which have a round handle, and ball valves, which have a lever. Ball valves are more reliable and can be turned off with a quarter turn.

The Quick Way to Stop the Water

For gate valves, turn the handle clockwise (right) several turns until it stops. For ball valves, turn the lever handle perpendicular to the pipe. Never force a stuck valve, since it can break and leave you worse off. Go to the meter and close it there with a meter key or an adjustable wrench.

Shutoff Valves at Each Fixture

A separate valve on most appliances and fixtures lets you cut water to just that spot while the rest of the house stays on. They are found near water heaters, behind washing machines and toilets, and under sinks. Knowing these locations allows you to stop localized leaks quickly while maintaining water service to the rest of your home.
