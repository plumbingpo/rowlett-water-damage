What Usually Goes Wrong in San Antonio Homes

Water usually escapes at the same few vulnerable spots in a home. These are the ones San Antonio property owners report to us again and again.

Water heaters: Sediment collects in the bottom of a tank and slowly eats through the steel. The first sign is often a rusty puddle or a damp ring on the floor around the base. If the tank ruptures, it keeps refilling and draining onto the floor until someone closes the cold supply valve. When the unit lives in an attic or interior closet, the water reaches carpets and ceilings far from it.

Roofs: Wind can lift shingles and drive rain underneath them in a single storm. Leaks also start around chimneys, vent pipes and skylights where the sealant has dried out. Water travels along rafters before it drips, so the wet spot inside may be far from the hole outside. On San Antonio roof leak calls, our crew removes saturated insulation, sets drying equipment and can cover the opening until a roofer arrives.

Freezing: Depending on the season, a hard overnight freeze can burst lines that were fine the day before. Outdoor spigots, sprinkler feeds and pipes in crawl spaces or along outside walls are hit first. Vacant homes and rooms with the heat turned down are especially exposed. No flow or a bare trickle at the tap on a frigid day means a frozen line, so close the main and get help.

Backups: A drain that gurgles or several fixtures that slow down together point to a blockage in the main line. Once it closes off fully, anything you flush or drain returns at the lowest opening in the house. Since the waste is hazardous, we extract it and then sanitize concrete, tile and other hard surfaces. Stay out of the affected rooms and avoid running water until the line is cleared.

Seepage: A wet line along the bottom of a foundation wall is a sign that water is building up in the soil next to it. The usual reasons are grading that tilts inward, clogged perimeter drains and downspouts that empty too close. Early clues include powdery white residue on the concrete, flaking wall paint and air that always smells musty.
