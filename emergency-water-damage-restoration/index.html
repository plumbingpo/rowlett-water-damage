Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.

Why Emergency Water Damage Restoration Is Not a DIY Job

The visible water is the easy part of an emergency. It is knowing where the rest of the water went. Baseboards, wall cavities and the space beneath flooring all take on water in minutes.
